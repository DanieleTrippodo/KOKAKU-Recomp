KOKAKU Recomp
Ghost in the Shell: Stand Alone Complex / USA ULUS10020

Native PC port by DDD
Support the project: https://ko-fi.com/X8X4FMCO5

Double-click KOKAKU Recomp.exe to play. Keep the EXE, DLL files and PSP_DATA
together. The original ISO and the setup utility are not needed to play.
You can move the entire folder to another location.

Windows x64 with DirectX 12 graphics support is required.
Settings are stored in KokakuNative.ini and KokakuControls.ini.
Saved games are stored in SAVEDATA beside the executable.

CONTROLS
Enter: Start. Arrow keys: menu navigation. Z: confirm. X: back.
WASD: move. Mouse: look. Left mouse: attack. E/right mouse: interact.
Space/Q: jump. R: reload. F: lock-on. Wheel: change weapon.
Esc: pause. F1: capture/release mouse. Alt+F4: close.
OPTION > KeyAssign > Z/Enter opens the PC settings.

MULTIPLAYER
Multi Play > Create opens a room. Multi Play > Join lists the rooms open on
the internet and on your local network: pick one with the arrow keys and Z.
Players connect directly to each other; allow the game through the Windows
firewall when asked. If a room cannot be reached, let the player with the
better connection host, or forward UDP port 3658 on the host's router.
Your player name: PC settings, NETWORK page. Everyone needs this same version.
When a connection fails, close the game and look at the lines starting with
"online:" in KokakuNative.log, next to the game's executable.
